## 1. Creative Philosophy & Visual Tone

Kalajay pieces are not mass-produced industrial items; they are hand-strung sculptures of light, patience, and touch. Every photograph must evoke:
- **Intimacy**: The feeling of holding a sacred treasure on your morning vanity table.
- **Material Authenticity**: The crisp light reflections off Czech faceted glass, the warm pearlescent luster of triple-coated pearls, and the taut strength of hand-knotted wire.
- **Human Connection**: Hands in frame, workshop textures, unhurried craftsmanship.

### Non-Negotiable Styling Rules
1. **Light Quality**: Always soft, diffused natural daylight (approx. 5200K–5600K) or gentle editorial continuous lighting. Never harsh direct flash or cold fluorescent studio lighting.
2. **Backdrops & Surfaces**: Pearl ivory linen, unpolished travertine stone, soft blush raw silk, textured handmade paper, and light warm oak. No pure sterile white plastic backdrops or dark vinyl.
3. **Props with Restraint**: When staging lifestyle sets, use subtle, authentic companions: vintage gold ring, brass hairpin, dried botanicals (e.g. baby's breath or jasmine buds), antique perfume atomizer. Never clutter the frame.
4. **No Digital Distortion**: No excessive AI generation, plastic smoothing filters, or unrealistic reflections. Let the subtle, charming micro-variations of hand-strung beads shine through.

### 03 · Product Detail Page (PDP) — The 5-Shot Mandatory Standard
Every product in the Kalajay catalogue requires exactly 5 cohesive photographs:

```
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│     Shot 01     │     Shot 02     │     Shot 03     │     Shot 04     │     Shot 05     │
│   Front Cover   │  Vanity Context │   Macro Detail  │ Capacity/Inside │  Human Scale    │
│  (Clean Studio) │   (Lifestyle)   │   (Light & Wire)│  (Utility Open) │  (Held in Hand) │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

1. **Shot 01 — Front Studio Profile (Cover Image)**:
   - *Composition*: Clean eye-level or slightly elevated 25-degree perspective.
   - *Background*: Soft `#FBF7F2` neutral gradient.
   - *Purpose*: Catalogue grid consistency and clear silhouette recognition.
2. **Shot 02 — Vanity Lifestyle Setting (Hover Swap Image)**:
   - *Composition*: Staged on a bedroom vanity table or marble console with warm morning light.
   - *Companions*: Beside a crystal perfume bottle or silk scrunchie.
   - *Purpose*: Provides emotional aspirational context for how the item lives in a home.
3. **Shot 03 — Macro Bead & Hardware Detail**:
   - *Composition*: 1:1 macro crop (100mm macro lens) focusing on the corner weave, clasp, or faceted bead reflection.
   - *Purpose*: Confirms the hand-strung wire tension and glass quality.
4. **Shot 04 — Interior & Utility View**:
   - *Composition*: Box or tote open, showing the velvet lining and demonstrating capacity (e.g. holding 3 rings, a lipstick, or credit cards).
   - *Purpose*: Answers practical customer questions regarding sizing.
5. **Shot 05 — Human Scale & Drape**:
   - *Composition*: Model holding the piece in palms or dangling tote from wrist.
   - *Wardrobe*: Neutral linen, soft cream knit, or pale pastel silk (no loud prints).
   - *Purpose*: Instantly communicates exact real-world dimensions.

## 4. On-Set Shoot Checklist

- [ ] Microfiber cleaning cloth to wipe dust or fingerprints off glass beads and crystal facets before every shutter press.
- [ ] Precision tweezer to ensure wire knots and closure hinges sit flush.
- [ ] Warm reflector (subtle gold/silver bounce) to fill shadow crevices under dark beads.
- [ ] Neutral cream cotton gloves for food/jewelry handling on set.
- [ ] Pre-steamed linen and cotton props with zero visible creases.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (React + Tailwind CSS)

## Users

Primary users are buyers seeking to discover, evaluate, and purchase products through an intuitive, trustworthy digital storefront.

## Product Purpose

Kalajay is an e-commerce platform engineered to provide a seamless end-to-end shopping experience, featuring curated catalog discovery, detailed product evaluation, and frictionless checkout.

## Positioning

A modern, high-craft e-commerce storefront blending visual elegance with speed, clarity, and dependable buyer workflows.

## Operating Context

Modern web browsers across desktop, tablet, and mobile touch devices. Key workflows include category exploration, search, variant inspection, cart management, and order completion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog browsing with category navigation, filtering, and search.
- Product detail views highlighting imagery, pricing, specs, and stock availability.
- Persistent shopping cart and transparent checkout flow.
- Fast, accessible layout conforming to modern web standards and mobile viewport constraints.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: Kalajay
- Visual Tone: Premium, dependable, and refined.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project root initialized at `kalajay-v2`.
- Storefront architecture, product models, inventory data, and payment providers to be wired during development.

## Product Principles

1. **Buyer Trust First**: Unambiguous pricing, explicit shipping/return expectations, and transparent transactional feedback.
2. **Effortless Discovery**: Instant search feedback, logical filtering, and clean information hierarchy.
3. **Ergonomic Mobility**: Touch targets exceeding 44×44px, thumb-friendly navigation, and zero layout shift.
4. **Performance & Speed**: Rapid asset loading, optimized image delivery, and smooth page transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
- Minimum 4.5:1 color contrast for normal text; 3:1 for large text and UI components.
- Complete keyboard navigability across navigation, cart drawers, modal dialogs, and form inputs.
- Clear ARIA labels for all icon-only actions and accessible error messaging on form inputs.
